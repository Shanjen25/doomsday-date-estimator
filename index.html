Doomsday Date Estimator

Toy probabilistic model · Not a prediction

Important: This is an educational toy using a constant annual hazard rate. Expert estimates vary widely and the future is not fixed. Humanity can (and should) work to lower these risks. See the GitHub repo for sources and the Python version.

Current value: 0.30% per year

How it works

Assuming a constant annual probability p of a civilization-ending (or near-ending) event:

  • Expected waiting time = 1 / p years
  • Median date (50% cumulative chance) = ln(2) / p years from now

Nuclear risk currently dominates most expert assessments compared with purely natural risks (asteroids, supervolcanoes, etc.). Numbers are drawn from public surveys and papers (Forecasting Research Institute, 80,000 Hours, Snyder-Beattie et al., etc.).
